# Project Overview

## Product

GearLead AI is an AI Sales Engineer Assistant for B2B gaming peripheral exporters. It supports the first-response workflow after an English inquiry is received.

## Objective

Reduce the time and inconsistency involved in inquiry reading, requirement extraction, catalog search, lead qualification, and reply preparation while keeping commercial decisions under human control.

## Primary User

An export salesperson handling gaming mice, mechanical keyboards, gaming headsets, custom cables, and custom keycaps.

## Inputs and Outputs

Inputs are pasted English text, TXT, or DOCX. Outputs are a validated inquiry record, missing-field list, customer/risk evidence, product candidates, match type, lead score, priority, follow-up plan, English reply draft, and optional local CRM record.

## Success Definition

The POC must run without an API key, initialize its own database, process 25 test inquiries, meet the PRD metric thresholds, and explain every business decision.

## Non-goals

The POC does not send mail, connect to a real CRM, determine final prices or delivery dates, perform legal/credit/compliance checks, train a model, or implement a multi-agent platform.
