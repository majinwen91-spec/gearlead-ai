# Business Background

Gaming peripheral export inquiries combine sales qualification and technical presales. A buyer may describe connection modes, sensors, layouts, certifications, materials, OEM packaging, annual volume, samples, and launch dates in a single unstructured email.

The first response requires nine decisions:

1. Identify the buyer and market.
2. Extract product and commercial requirements.
3. Identify missing information.
4. Assess explicit risk evidence.
5. Search the relevant catalog category.
6. Determine standard, light-custom, or ODM fit.
7. Check MOQ and commercial potential.
8. Select a follow-up route.
9. Draft a response without over-committing.

The workflow is a good AI solution candidate because language interpretation is probabilistic, while catalog facts, scoring, thresholds, and approval boundaries are deterministic. GearLead AI combines both instead of asking one model to make the entire decision.
